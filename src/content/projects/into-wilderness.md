The performance *Into the Wilderness* originates in a privately administered natural conservation space in the Caucasus Wildlife Refuge, which includes the development of tourism along with several other programs.

The artist moves one of the plants located at the Refuge to a place beyond the borders of the Refuge — to the banks of a river — thus moving it across three conventional borders: the first is the border of the Refuge itself, the second is its entry into private territory, and the third is at the edge of the road belonging to the local community. Thus, the plant finds itself in an emergency, raising questions on the thinking behind the planning of nature, its commoditization and demarcation, as well as serving as a metaphor for forced migration.

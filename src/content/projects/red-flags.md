In this intervention, the artist appropriates the facade of a former Soviet factory, known as "Impulse." Red flags, emblematic of the Soviet era, are placed in the original flag holders, evoking a visual continuity with the historical context. This subtle yet deliberate act prompts contemplation on the passage of time and the transformation of symbolic spaces within the trajectory of political ideologies.

A tribute to Mladen Stilinović. Banner, 2016.

The unfurling banner commands attention with its stark simplicity, boldly displaying a timeless message: "Activist who cannot speak English is no activist." This piece pays homage to the seminal artist Mladen Stilinović and reinterprets his iconic declaration, infusing it with renewed relevance.

Reflecting the essence of Stilinović's body of work, the unembellished typography adheres faithfully to the original color palette — a visual tribute to the provocative aesthetics of the artist. However, a subtle modification replaces "artist" with "activist," introducing a nuanced alteration that encourages a fresh interpretation of the artist's thesis within the context of socio-political engagement.

In this artistic endeavor, the creator, akin to a semiotic trickster, prompts critical inquiry, urging viewers to explore the intricate interplay of language, power dynamics, and the politics of identity. The piece invites contemplation on the dual role of language as both a bridge and a barrier in activism — an exploration that challenges preconceptions about what defines an activist in a world where language serves as both a tool and a threshold.

This homage to Stilinović's original declaration initiates a dialogical narrative, forging a connection between the realms of art and activism. It serves as a reminder that within the diverse tapestry of human endeavors, the potency of words endures as a conduit for transformation and an invitation to participate in the discourse of change.

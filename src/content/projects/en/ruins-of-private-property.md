Video is a collection of still filmings in the Sarıyer district in Istanbul. Construction at the Uyum development in Sarıyer was halted by the municipality in 1987 on grounds of illegally obtaining building permits, but the actual demolition of the site is being carried out now, after 20 years of the actual ceasing of the operation. So far in the demolition process, 57 villas have been brought down.
